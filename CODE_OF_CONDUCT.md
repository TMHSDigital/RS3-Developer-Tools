# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the players who use these plugins

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Submitting plugins or tooling intended to cheat, exploit, or harm other players, or encouraging others to do so

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported through a GitHub issue marked `conduct` or privately through a GitHub Security Advisory on this repository. Maintainers will review and respond within 7 days and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code.
